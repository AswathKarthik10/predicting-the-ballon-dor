# v2.1 deviations (awards fix spec v2.1). EXPLORATORY, post-hoc.

Everything in v2.1 is a correction made after the 2026 result was seen. The v2 forecast (tag `forecast-v2`, `results/v2/`) is unchanged. v2.1 is never presented as the forecast.

1. **F11 redefined after the 2026 result was known.** Reasons (spec section 0): second and third places outscored wins (Messi's silver Golden Ball 1.5 vs Rodri's gold 1); every award weighed the same; one achievement stacked (Mbappe's World Cup counted twice); z-scoring a 0-3.5 count gave two players about 3 sd. The weights (type 1.0 / 0.6 / 0.3, place 1 / 0.5 / 0.25, difficulty in [0.1, 1.0]) were fixed in the spec before any v2.1 run and are not changed.
2. **League top scorers added after seeing Kane score zero in v2.** Justified by symmetry with tournament top scorers; S1 reports the result without them.
3. **The v2 spec's own error:** the formula's "+0.5 for a 2nd or 3rd-place Golden Ball (gold already counted)" assumed the count held winners only; the dataset counts any place.
4. **Order of work.** Part A (dataset) and the B3 back-test ran before the v2 comparison with the actual 2026 result (spec section 4, step 2) because `data/holdout/bdo_2026_result.csv` had not been supplied. The back-test uses no 2026 outcome. The v2 comparison must be committed before B5/B6.
5. **Part A build decisions** (details in the dataset repo's `docs/build_notes.md`, Part 27, commit d174272, dataset repo commit pinned in `environment.txt`): league top scorers from league list pages, not season pages; MLS `announced_date` 31 December (not 31 May); Eredivisie scorers left blank; the Gold Cup spot fact replaced by "the 2022 and 2026 World Cups at least as strong as every Gold Cup" (no Gold Cup is played in those years); Elo of the day before a tournament taken as the morning of its first match; league strength uses the rating captured once per season (2026: 2026-04-16).
6. **Season labels.** League awards use "25/26" and league scorers "2025/26"; both are normalised to the season's end year so that a league's player award and top-scorer award of one season form one competition-season for the one-event rule.
7. **Code.** `FeatureOptions` gained an `awards` field (None keeps the v2 count); `run_stage3.main` was split into `backtest` + `main` so the v2.1 runner reuses it with the same folds, grids, seeds and models. With `awards=None` the v2 numbers are reproduced (checked below).
8. **B5 run before the v2 comparison and before the B3 review sign-off.** At the user's explicit instruction (the user compares the forecast with the actual result themselves), `run_v2_1_forecast.py` was run before the v2 comparison of experiments spec 9.3 was committed and before `data/holdout/bdo_2026_result.csv` was supplied. No 2026 outcome was read (the forecast guard found no result file). S1-S4 top-5 runs reuse the main run's juror bias and link; only the final fit and the utilities change.
